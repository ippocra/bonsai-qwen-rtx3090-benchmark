# Saturated Margin

Saturated Margin treats hardware capacity as a physical landscape: dense, luminous blocks press against the edge of a measured field, while quiet negative space marks the room left for systems to breathe. The work communicates through load, boundary, and release. It avoids decorative explanation and lets proportion do the argument. Every bar, gridline, and label must feel meticulously crafted, as if refined through many careful passes by a practitioner at the top of their field.

Color is a language of thermal pressure. Bonsai lives in a cool electric green that signals efficient compression and spare headroom; Qwen occupies a saturated violet-blue that carries weight and ambition. OOM is not merely red, but a precise fault line: a thin, intentional incision through the diagram. The palette is limited so every contrast has purpose. The final artifact must show painstaking attention to chromatic balance, with no casual defaults or accidental emphasis.

The composition uses engineering restraint rather than marketing noise. Space, alignment, and repetition create the sense of an instrument panel for a serious local-AI lab. Text is sparse and functional: model names, memory figures, boundary notes, and concise conclusions only. The infographics should appear like evidence from a carefully run experiment, not a slide made after the fact.

Craftsmanship is the central ethic. Each canvas should look labored over with care: aligned to invisible grids, balanced in weight, readable from a distance, and still rewarding close inspection. The benchmark's story is told by the shape of the data: full context, full offload, VRAM ceiling, KV precision, and quality score. The design succeeds when the viewer understands the conclusion before reading a sentence.
